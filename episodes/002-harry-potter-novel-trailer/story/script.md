# Episode 002: HARRY POTTER — Geometries of the Novel (Cinematic Trailer Remix)

## Concept & Literary Direction
A 16-second, 4-act procedural 3D cinematic trailer remix inspired directly by the prose imagery of J.K. Rowling's novel *Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone*—reimagined through **Headless Blender 4.2 Cycles raytracing** and **procedural audio synthesis** without using a single frame from the commercial films.
